Threadly is a daily word-guessing puzzle.

Each day brings one new puzzle. You get up to five clues, revealed one at a time. Guess the answer at any point: the fewer clues you use, the more points you score, from 100 points for a first-clue solve down to 20 points when all five clues are out.

- One new puzzle every day, resetting at 00:00 UTC
- Forgiving answers: close spellings and plurals count
- Personal stats and daily streaks on your device
- Share your result card with friends without spoiling the answer
- No ads and no account sign-up

Threadly needs an internet connection to fetch each day's puzzle and check your guess.
